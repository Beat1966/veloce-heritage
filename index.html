The most beautiful race in the world, from the best seat in the car.

Buying a car eligible for the Mille Miglia is a serious decision, and renting one to drive yourself means learning a race you have never seen. There is a gentler way in.

With VELOCE heritage you ride as co-driver. You read the roadbook, keep the time, wave to the crowds that line the streets of every town between Brescia and Rome, and live four days inside the event. Beat Sutter drives, and his team handles the car, the paperwork and the logistics.

Afterwards you will know whether this is your world. You might go on to buy a car of your own, rent one and drive yourself, or simply keep the memory. All three are good outcomes.

From first call to the finish line in Brescia

There is nothing you need to organise. This is how a reservation unfolds.

  1. A short introductory call

    Fifteen minutes to get to know each other, at a time that suits your time zone.

  2. A detailed conversation

    About twenty-five minutes on the event, the car, travel and what you would like from the week.

  3. Your written offer

    A private proposal by email. If it suits you, you confirm and make the first partial payment.

  4. We handle the entry

    Online registration, fees, FIVA documentation and the technical inspections. You sign one entrant declaration, nothing more. The second payment is due before the entry is submitted.

  5. You arrive in Italy

    You are collected, briefed on the roadbook and timing, fitted into the car and introduced to the team.

  6. Four days on the road

    Some 1,600 km of historic Italian roads, with a support crew and mechanics following the route.

Your pilot, Beat Sutter

Beat has been part of the 1000 Miglia since 2001: five times behind the wheel, and fifteen more as the man responsible for teams’ mechanics and route logistics.

A classic car collector from Thun, Switzerland, he has driven and looked after historic automobiles since 1989. He is a member of the Club 1000 Miglia Franco Mazzotti in Brescia, a scrutineer for the Swiss Historic Vehicle Federation, and board member and technical expert of the Oberländer Auto-Freunde.

In 2025 he also completed the motorcycle edition of the race, Milano–Taranto, on a 1956 Vespa 150 “Struzzo”: more than 1,800 km on two wheels.

The cars

Four historic automobiles, each with a fully documented history and FIVA classification.

Fiat 1100 S, 1949

Carrozzerie Speciali Fiat, FIVA class B

A post-war racing car from Turin, built for long-distance races. It started the original 1000 Miglia in 1949 and finished in the top ten at the 1950 Coppa InterEuropa in Monza. In 1952 it received a hand-built prototype body from the Fiat 8V pre-series.

Its owners included 1000 Miglia winner Flaminio Valseriati, collector Mario Righini and Gino Danieli, co-founder of the modern Mille Miglia revival in Brescia. It has started the modern 1000 Miglia in 2000, 2001 and 2015.

Period-correct Fiat 1100 S engine, 51 hp, top speed 150 km/h

Questions guests usually ask

Do I need racing or rally experience?

No. You will be briefed before the start on the roadbook, the timing and how a day on the route works. Navigating is learned quickly, and Beat guides you through every stage.

What does a seat cost?

Pricing is private and depends on the event, the car and the hospitality you would like. You receive a written offer after our second call. Payment is made in two instalments.

I live in the US or Australia. How does travel work?

You book your flight to northern Italy; from the moment you land, we take over. We collect you, arrange hotels along the route and take you back afterwards. Calls are scheduled to suit your time zone.

Can I bring a partner?

The car has one co-driver seat, but a companion can follow parts of the route and join the evening events. Let us know and we will include it in your offer.

What happens if the car has a technical problem?

A support vehicle with specialist mechanics and spare parts follows the route. Beat has managed the mechanics for 1000 Miglia teams for more than twenty years.

And if I want my own car afterwards?

We are happy to advise. Beat has bought, prepared and entered historic cars for clients and knows which models are eligible and what to look for.

For brands and partners

A historic racing car at the 1000 Miglia draws the attention of a very particular audience. We work with a small number of partners as official technical partner (tyres, oils, tools) or as heritage partner, with photo shoots, client hospitality and paddock access before, during and after the race.
